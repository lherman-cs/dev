# Session goal routing

`/dev-spec`, `/dev-build`, and `/dev-review` (including `/skill:` forms) activate a session goal. `/dev-goal start` activates one explicitly; ship does not. The original request and direct human clarifications define its scope. Todo is the foreground's checklist, not assessor input. Phase skills retain their own evidence and approval gates.

At a whole-goal stopping point, `stopping_report` supplies `remaining` (concise text, `unknown`, or null), `nextAction` (concise actionable text or null), `dependency` (kind `none`, `human`, `external`, or `unknown`, with detail for non-none), and `complete` (explicit whole-goal assertion, including applicable approvals). The isolated assessor sees only this report, no transcript or tools, and returns exactly `CONTINUE`, `WAIT`, or `COMPLETE`. It routes the foreground's assertions, not correctness. A known repair or viable alternative is CONTINUE; missing approval or ambiguity is WAIT. Contradictory decisions cannot turn an unfinished or unknown goal into completion. Invalid transport and assessor failure stop automatic execution with visible errors, not a retry loop or tool lock.

CONTINUE schedules a fixed in-scope foreground turn. WAIT identifies a dependency or asks for clarification without scheduling; a human reply may permit further interaction without a separate resume and is never inferred to be approval. Relevant external results may wake a waiting goal. Explicit human pause (including Escape) stops that goal until explicit resume; abandonment ends it. Goal status does not revoke ordinary tool permissions. Late results cannot override pause, abandonment, or replacement. On restoration, an unfinished goal remains stopped until the human resumes it.

In a TUI, bare `/dev-goal` opens a read-only, branch-local Goal Hub. It lists the latest goal first, previews progress on wide terminals, and opens a timestamped event timeline with Enter. Use arrows or the mouse to choose goals, Enter to inspect an event's assessor input/output and runtime action, PageUp/PageDown to read long details, F1 for help, and Escape to go back or close. `/dev-goal show` displays text status (also the fallback outside the TUI). `/dev-goal pause`, `resume`, `abandon`, and `start <request>` are human controls; `goal_control` interprets direct human instructions. Side questions do not enlarge scope. Ambiguous replacement asks the human first. Opening the hub does not control execution; historical records show unavailable markers for missing legacy evidence.
