# Completion guard

`/dev-build` and `/skill:dev-build` automatically activate a session-native goal. `/dev-goal start` activates one explicitly; spec and ship do not activate goals. The original request and direct human clarifications define its scope. Todo remains the foreground's step-level checklist, not classifier input.

The foreground can declare a terminal `complete` or `blocked` outcome via `finish` (summary and evidence are foreground assertions). These dispositions settle directly, without independent correctness certification. The foreground remains responsible for the invoked skill's verification, review, and human gates. `continue_goal` explicitly states that work remains and schedules a fixed continuation without classification.

At an ambiguous normal stopping point, the foreground may call `stopping_report` with exactly `progress` (nonempty short text), `remaining` (nonempty short text or null), and `blocker` (nonempty short text or null). A null remaining value means no remaining work; use `"unknown"` for uncertainty. Invalid input produces a corrective tool error before classification. The configured `assessor` role resolves a single isolated LLM call over only the three-field report, with no tools or inherited task context. It must return exactly `done`, `continue`, `blocked`, or `unclear`. This narrow label describes the foreground's stopping intent, not whether its work is correct. `done` and `blocked` settle the reported outcome, `continue` schedules one fixed continuation, and `unclear` pauses. Model failure, timeout, and invalid output also pause without retrying or fabricating a report. A final answer without a report or explicit disposition pauses, unless a known current-goal worker result is still pending.

`/dev-goal` displays status and stage history, including the resolved assessor model and thinking setting. `/dev-goal pause`, `resume`, `abandon`, and `start <request>` are direct human controls; `goal_control` exposes them to the foreground only for a direct human instruction. Side questions never expand the goal. Replacement of an unfinished goal asks the human first. Pending worker deliveries and repeated continuation without progress retain their bounded safety checks. Pausing, navigation, replacement, and process restoration invalidate pending classification; historical stage events remain historical. A later request does not retroactively change a completed goal.
