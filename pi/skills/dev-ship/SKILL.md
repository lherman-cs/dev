# dev-ship

Read `../../references/reconcile.md` at entry. Read `../../references/engineering.md` at entry. Reconstruct the intended outcome, fixed local comparison base and exact candidate scope, history, validation, prior review coverage, and unresolved feedback. Include uncommitted candidate changes. A resumed session alone does not justify repeating a reviewer launch: reuse evidence still applicable to the candidate.

Own the local candidate through a collaborative conversation:

1. **Open with a living review brief.** Inspect and judge the candidate yourself. Give a compact, decision-ready account of the practical outcome (a concrete before/after when helpful), consequential choices and rationale, supporting evidence and its limits, important risks or tradeoffs, and your recommendation or matters requiring human judgment. Scale depth to the change; organize by meaning rather than files, commits, or process. Take a position while distinguishing verified facts, assumptions, and unknowns. Do not imply readiness beyond available evidence. The human should understand the change, why it is sensible, and where it could fail without reconstructing the diff.

2. **Discuss and repair.** Follow the human's questions or redirects, investigating scenarios, architecture, or code as needed without restarting a tour. Keep track of unresolved concerns and resurface consequential ones before closing. Ask only when a real decision requires human input; explain its concrete consequences and recommend a direction where warranted. Do not create routine continuation prompts or section-by-section checkpoints. Fix clear in-scope defects directly. Discuss consequential changes to agreed behavior, scope, or risk with the human before implementing them. Reject unsupported, speculative, taste-only, unrelated, redundant, or low-value churn. Validate affected behavior; report material changes to behavior, risk, evidence, or the assessment without repeating the whole brief each time. Reopen an earlier decision only when new evidence or changes materially affect its premise. Do not demand approval for routine repairs.

3. **Review the settled candidate.** After discussion and known repairs settle, use the effort's single broad `review` on the stable agreed candidate, supplying the intended outcome, fixed base, exact candidate fingerprint and applicable evidence. Freeze a concise whole-scope coverage assignment and the highest-risk invariants deserving deep adversarial attention. Require one batched result that accounts for every focus, gives concrete closure checks for each finding, and discloses unexamined coverage rather than stopping at the first defect. Do not review a moving target. Treat findings as evidence, not approval: adjudicate them, repair and verify accepted defects yourself, inspect affected changes, and reopen only the relevant human discussion when a finding changes an agreed decision or reveals important risk. Material repairs invalidate affected validation; obtain fresh applicable proof rather than treating earlier checks as covering changed behavior. The Shipper owns routine finding closure, so repairs do not require a Reviewer PASS.

   Use the effort's one repair audit only when subtle interactions, weak executable proof, or substantial redesign leave a concrete independent coverage need. Explain that need and obtain human permission first, then freeze the audit to the accepted finding keys, repair delta and directly affected invariants. It is not another whole-candidate review and cannot create an audit loop. Resolve and disclose anything left after it without automatically launching another reviewer. Reuse the effort's durable review receipts after resumption and reuse still-applicable coverage across history-only commit changes.

4. **Confirm, then refine.** Once meaningful findings are resolved and required validation passes, present the consolidated brief for the actual resulting candidate: outcome, meaningful verification, remaining risks, and final recommendation. Explicitly ask the human whether review is complete and meaningful feedback is exhausted. Do not infer convergence from silence. Only then create a recoverable pre-cleanup reference and rewrite the owned candidate history into the smallest useful set of coherent Conventional Commits. Preserve the comparison base and unrelated/shared history; verify tree equivalence and the final base-to-candidate diff. History-only changes preserving the reviewed tree do not require another human approval or behavioral revalidation solely because commit IDs changed. Leave a clean worktree and present a concise closing view of behavior, decisions and repairs, validation, residual risks, and final commits.

Keep independent review and validation asynchronous against an exact candidate. Continue useful work while they run, but do not change the tested candidate until verification completes or is cancelled; wait when evidence gates progress.

**Endpoint:** A committed local candidate with an intentional diff, coherent history, applicable passing validation, no known meaningful review feedback, and explicit human convergence. The human owns branch integration and remote publishing. Do not fetch, push, mutate remote review state, or merge into another branch.
