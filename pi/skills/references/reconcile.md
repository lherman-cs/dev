# Reconcile before acting

Work from observable state, even with no prior session. Read the current request, approved artifacts, Git history/status/diff (including untracked files), and relevant remote evidence. Compare completed work and current proof with the phase's remaining obligations. Prior transcripts and worker results are leads, not proof. Verify uncertain side effects before retrying; do not assume an interrupted child still runs or that its verdict applies to a changed candidate. Ask when intent, approval, identity, or exclusive writing ownership cannot be established.

Own the exclusive worktree: inspect and classify pre-existing tracked and untracked changes before staging or removal. Incorporate relevant edits only under the same scope and proof as new work. Preserve relevant conflicts for a human decision. For irrelevant edits, attempt a recoverable local backup outside the worktree, then remove only the classified paths; report paths removed and backup location or failure. Backup is best-effort, not a veto, and may be unverified. Never sweep ignored files, rewrite committed history, overwrite independent remote work, or mutate while another writer owns the tree. Continue proven work; pause with a concrete choice on repeated nonproductive failure or missing evidence.
